Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 20, 2026

Welcome to our StatUnitiii Mobile App. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, and share your personal information when you use our mobile application, services, features, and website (collectively, the "Services").

By accessing or using the Services, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, your choice is not to use our Services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our Services and features, we must process information about you. The types of information we collect depend on how you use our Services.

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your username, email address, password, date of birth, and phone number.
  • Profile Information: You may choose to provide a profile picture, bio, website link, and other details to complete your profile.
  • User Content: The photos, videos, audio, comments, and other materials you create, upload, or share on the app. We also collect metadata associated with this content (e.g., when and where a photo was taken).
  • Communications: Messages you send to other users, feedback, and support inquiries.

B. Information We Collect Automatically

  • Device and Usage Information: We collect information about how you use our Services, such as the types of content you view or engage with, the features you use, the actions you take, and the time, frequency, and duration of your activities.
  • Device Attributes: Information such as the operating system, hardware and software versions, battery level, signal strength, available storage space, browser type, app and file names and types, and plugins.
  • Network and Connections: Information such as your IP address, mobile operator or ISP, language, time zone, and connection speed.

C. Device Permissions

To use specific features, we request permission to access certain capabilities on your device. You can manage these in your device settings:

  • Camera & Microphone: To allow you to take photos, record videos, and capture audio directly within the app.
  • Photo Library (Gallery): To allow you to upload existing photos and videos from your device.
  • Storage: To save media to your device and cache data for faster loading.
  • Location Services: We collect precise or approximate location data from your device if you permit us to do so, to tag locations on your posts or show you local content.
  • Contacts: If you sync your contacts, we access them to help you find friends who are already using our app.
  • Notifications: To send you alerts about new followers, likes, comments, and messages.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we have for the following purposes:

  • Provide and Improve the Services: To operate our app, deliver the features you request, and develop new products and features.
  • Personalize Your Experience: To show you a personalized feed, recommend accounts you may want to follow, and tailor content and advertisements to your interests.
  • Communicate With You: To send you marketing communications, respond to your inquiries, and inform you about our policies and terms.
  • Promote Safety, Integrity, and Security: To verify accounts and activity, combat harmful conduct, detect and prevent spam and other bad experiences, maintain the integrity of our Services, and promote safety and security on and off our Services.
  • Measurement and Analytics: To understand how people use our Services so we can improve them and provide reporting to partners.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following ways:

  • With Other Users: Content you post on your public profile is visible to everyone. If your account is private, it is visible only to your approved followers.
  • Service Providers: We share information with vendors and service providers who support our business, such as cloud hosting services, content delivery networks, and analytics providers.
  • Advertising Partners: We may partner with third-party advertising networks to show you relevant ads. They may use cookies and similar technologies to collect information about your activities over time.
  • Legal Requests and Prevention of Harm: We may access, preserve, and share your information in response to a legal request if we have a good-faith belief that the law requires us to do so, or to detect, prevent, and address fraud, unauthorized use of the Services, violations of our Terms or Policies, or other harmful or illegal activity.

4. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under applicable privacy laws, including:

  • Access and Portability: The right to request access to and a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: The right to request that we update or correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Deletion: The right to request that we delete your personal information.
  • Opt-Out: You can opt out of receiving marketing emails by clicking the "unsubscribe" link. You can also manage your device permissions to restrict our access to your camera, location, and contacts.

5. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as we need it to provide you with the Services or until you delete your account, whichever comes first. When you delete your account, we delete things you have posted, such as your photos and status updates, and you will not be able to recover that information later.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete that information as quickly as possible.
